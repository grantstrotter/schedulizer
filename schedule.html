Schedulizer Guide

Getting started

Start a new project, open a saved .json file, or drag one onto the opening screen. Your work auto-saves in this browser as you go, but that's just a safety net for refreshes — use Save (or Cmd/Ctrl+S) to actually export a .json file you can keep or reopen later.

Days

Seven columns across the top, one per day, each with a Groups section and a People section. You can drop a group or person anywhere in that day's column — not just its matching section — and it'll land in the right spot.

Groups

+ Add Group creates one in the Groups drawer below the days. Click the ✎ icon to rename it, drag it by its title bar onto any day to schedule it there, and drag it back to the drawer to unschedule it.

People

Bring people in with Import People (CSV) (columns: first,last,phone,email,isLeader,availability — availability is optional, a |-separated list of days like sunday|thursday or Sun|Thu) or add them one at a time with + Add Leader / + Add Participant. Click a person's name to expand their card and fill in contact info. Drag a person's title bar onto a day, or straight into a group to nest them under it.

Availability

Inside an expanded person card, check the nights they're available. A person with no boxes checked is treated as available every night. Trying to drop someone (or a group, based on its leaders) on a night they're not available is blocked, with a toast explaining why.

Leader ⇄ Participant

Toggle the Leader checkbox on an expanded person card to flip their role — if they're unassigned it also moves them to the other drawer; if they're already placed on a day or in a group, they just change roles in place.

Exporting

Save downloads the full project as JSON (reopen it anytime). Export CSV downloads a flattened, spreadsheet-friendly view of the schedule — one row per assigned person, plus a trailing block of anyone still unassigned.
