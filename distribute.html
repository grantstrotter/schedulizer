Schedulizer Distribute Guide

What this tool is for

Once you've decided which nights your groups run (using the Schedule tool), use this page to place participants into those groups based on their ranked preferences from a sign-up form.

Getting started

Start a new project, open a saved .json file, or import a sign-up CSV exported from Google Forms — that import creates your groups automatically, one per group column in the form. Your work auto-saves in this browser as a safety net, but use Save (or Cmd/Ctrl+S) to export a .json file you can keep or reopen later.

Importing a sign-up CSV

Export responses from your Google Form as CSV. This tool looks for columns whose header contains a group name in brackets, e.g. Which groups would you prefer to do? [Wednesday Night - Young Adults], with values like 1st Choice, 2nd Choice, etc. One group is created per such column — even if nobody ranked it — and the night is parsed out of the group's name when it starts with a day name, purely for availability checking. It also expects a Name column (split on the first space into first/last), and optionally Email, Phone, and an availability column (comma-separated day names). Importing again adds new people; a row whose email matches an existing leader merges into that leader instead of creating a duplicate.

Groups

Groups sit side-by-side on the board — there's no day grid here, since a group's night was already decided elsewhere. Each group card shows how many people are in it against its minimum. Drag participants and leaders directly into a group, or back out to the drawers.

Preference match

Each participant shows a colored number badge next to their name — their current group's rank in their preferences: blue for a great match (1st choice), shading through green, yellow, and orange down to red for a poor one (5th choice or worse). A dark red ! badge means they're in a group they didn't rank at all (a likely problem worth a second look). It recomputes live as you move people between groups.

Placing by preference

The Auto-Assign… menu runs one placement step at a time, so you can check the board between steps. Place 1st Choices puts everyone still unassigned into their 1st choice group, unconditionally. Each Fill Under-Minimum: Nth Choices step then pulls people whose Nth choice is an under-filled group in from wherever they're currently sitting, preferring to pull from whichever group has the biggest surplus over its minimum first — run 2nd, then 3rd, and so on as needed. Any step can be re-run any time (e.g. after manual adjustments). Leaders are never moved by these steps — they're placed by hand and stay put. Anything still under-filled afterward is left for you to adjust.

Leader ⇄ Participant

Toggle the Leader checkbox on an expanded person card to flip their role, same as the scheduling tool.

Exporting

Save downloads the full project as JSON. Export CSV downloads one row per person with their group, preference rank, and match emoji.
